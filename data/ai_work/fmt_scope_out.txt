# Painting Proposal

**Project:** Planet Fitness  
**Contractor:** WL Painting Inc.

## Scope of Work

1. Provide labor and materials to prep and paint PT-1 GWB walls with eggshell finish, approximately 1,200 SF.
2. Provide labor and materials to prep and paint ceilings with flat finish, approximately 800 SF.
3. Provide labor and materials to prep and paint doors and frames with semi-gloss finish.

## Clarifications

1. Work to be performed during regular business hours.
2. Paint products shall be Benjamin Moore or equal, applied per manufacturer recommendations.
3. Wall finish shall be eggshell on GWB; ceiling finish shall be flat; doors and frames shall be semi-gloss.

## Exclusions

- Exterior painting unless specifically noted
- Work outside regular hours unless otherwise agreed
- Surfaces or areas not identified in the scope above
