# Painting plan-sweep checklist

## Painting plan-sweep checklist

### General
- [ ] Confirm paint scope vs. finish schedule (AF010) and Finish Plan (A2.01) ΓÇö no orphan rooms without a finish code
- [ ] Verify all room tags on A1.01 Floor Plan map to a finish type on A2.01 / AF010
- [ ] Cross-check elevations (A4.01) for painted surfaces not coded on the finish plan (bulkheads, soffits, feature walls)
- [ ] Note which surfaces are owner/vendor graphics vs. field paint (Planet Fitness brand accents)
- [ ] Confirm substrate types (GWB, CMU, metal, concrete, wood) before assigning systems
- [ ] Check for paint exclusion notes (prefinished, FRP, tile, stainless, aluminum storefront)
- [ ] Confirm primer requirements by substrate and existing vs. new construction
- [ ] Verify VOC / LEED or local Fredericksburg/VA coatings requirements if specified
- [ ] Identify touch-up vs. full field paint for TI vs. shell conditions
- [ ] Confirm whether ceilings, deck, structure, and MEP are painted or left unpainted

### Interior
- [ ] Fitness floor perimeter walls ΓÇö field color + brand accent walls (purple / yellow / black zones typical for PF)
- [ ] Cardio / strength / 30-minute circuit zone accent walls and column wraps
- [ ] Front desk / Black Card / sales office walls and accent panels
- [ ] Locker rooms (menΓÇÖs / womenΓÇÖs / family) ΓÇö moisture-resistant or epoxy system above tile/FRP
- [ ] Toilet rooms / wet walls ΓÇö paint above wainscot vs. full-height paint
- [ ] Tanning / HydroMassage / Total Body Enhancement rooms (typical PF amenities)
- [ ] Group fitness / stretch / free-weight rooms
- [ ] Offices, break room, storage, laundry
- [ ] Electrical, data, mechanical, janitor rooms (often missed on finish plans)
- [ ] Corridors, vestibule, exit passages
- [ ] Interior columns, pilasters, and pipe wraps in open fitness volume
- [ ] Exposed structure / open ceiling (deck, joists, duct, conduit) if schedule calls for paint
- [ ] Softfits, bulkheads, ceiling clouds, and gypsum ceilings
- [ ] Stair stringers, risers, landings, and stair walls (if multi-level or mezzanine)
- [ ] Handrails / guardrails field paint vs. prefinished
- [ ] Access panels, FDC cabinets, fire extinguisher cabinets (to match adjacent wall)
- [ ] Hollow metal / GWB chase walls at equipment and restrooms

### Exterior
- [ ] Exterior wall paint / EIFS / stucco / CMU field and accent colors (A4.01 elevations)
- [ ] Canopy underside, fascia, and metal panel returns
- [ ] Storefront framing ΓÇö field paint vs. anodized/prefinished (usually excluded; confirm)
- [ ] Exterior hollow metal doors and frames
- [ ] Bollards, pipe guards, and utility screens
- [ ] Exposed steel, lintels, and miscellaneous metals
- [ ] Roof screen / RTU screening if painted
- [ ] Exterior stair / ramp steel (if any)
- [ ] Signage backing panels or painted wall areas behind monument/building signs
- [ ] Dumpster enclosure CMU/steel (often paint-miss)

### Doors & frames
- [ ] All HM door frames ΓÇö prime + finish (typical full paint scope)
- [ ] HM doors ΓÇö paint both faces and edges
- [ ] Wood doors ΓÇö factory finish vs. field paint/stain (confirm AF010)
- [ ] Vision lite kits / sidelites ΓÇö paint vs. prefinished
- [ ] Door frames at restrooms, mechanical, electrical, storage (easy misses)
- [ ] Exterior HM doors ΓÇö exterior enamel system
- [ ] Overhead / coiling door frames and surrounds if present
- [ ] Frame-to-wall caulk coordination (painter vs. sealant scope)

### Specialty
- [ ] Epoxy / high-performance coatings in wet areas, janitor, laundry
- [ ] Anti-graffiti or cleanable coatings at high-traffic PF public walls (if specified)
- [ ] Floor striping / court or functional zone lines (if in paint scope vs. flooring)
- [ ] Painted graphics, logos, or brand murals ΓÇö field paint underlay vs. vinyl wrap
- [ ] Mirror wall backers / GWB behind full-height mirrors (paint before install)
- [ ] Equipment wall protection vs. paint interface
- [ ] Intumescent fireproofing topcoat (if structural steel requires it)
- [ ] Mechanical/electrical equipment bases or housekeeping pads (if painted)
- [ ] Pipe identification / color coding (if required by spec)

### Wallcovering
- [ ] Confirm wallcovering vs. paint on AF010 / A2.01 (PF often limited WC)
- [ ] Vinyl / fabric WC at offices or feature walls ΓÇö primer/sealer by painter
- [ ] Wallcovering adhesive and primer responsibility
- [ ] Painted borders / reveals around wallcovering panels
- [ ] Removal/prep of existing WC if remodel (not stated ΓÇö confirm)

### Site
- [ ] Site light poles / bases ΓÇö paint vs. galvanized (if in set)
- [ ] Site railing, fences, gates
- [ ] Exterior concrete stain/sealer (if in paint division)
- [ ] Parking / curb paint (usually civil/striping ΓÇö confirm exclusion)
- [ ] Bike racks, benches, site furnishings field paint

## Likely finish systems

| Area | Likely system (infer from PF / commercial fitness) |
|------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Open fitness GWB walls | Latex eggshell/satin, 1 primer + 2 finish; brand accent colors |
| Offices / corridors | Latex eggshell, standard commercial |
| Locker / toilet (above tile/FRP) | Moisture-resistant acrylic or eggshell epoxy-alternative |
| Janitor / laundry / wet support | Semi-gloss acrylic or waterborne epoxy |
| HM doors & frames | Alkyd or waterborne DTM semi-gloss over shop primer |
| Exterior CMU/EIFS/stucco | Elastomeric or 100% acrylic exterior; masonry primer on CMU |
| Exterior metals / bollards | DTM acrylic or alkyd enamel |
| Exposed structure (if painted) | Dryfall or acrylic on deck/joists; coordinate overspray |
| Feature / brand accent walls | Same latex system, scheduled accent color(s) from finish schedule |
| Mech / elec rooms | Flat or eggshell utility paint |

## Missing sheets / RFI questions

1. **Full specification book / Division 09** ΓÇö Is painting specified (e.g., 09 91 00)? Only finish schedule AF010 is listed; need product manufacturers, sheens, and MPI/OTC systems.
2. **Room finish schedule completeness** ΓÇö Does AF010 list every room on A1.01, including mech, elec, data, janitor, and storage?
3. **Reflected ceiling plan** ΓÇö No RCP named in set list; are open ceilings / deck / ductwork painted?
4. **Interior elevations / enlarged restroom plans** ΓÇö Beyond A4.01, are wet-area paint limits (above tile/FRP) detailed?
5. **Door schedule** ΓÇö Confirm HM vs. wood, factory vs. field finish for all doors/frames.
6. **Exterior material notes** ΓÇö Confirm painted surfaces vs. prefinished metal panels / storefront on A4.01.
7. **Brand standards package** ΓÇö Planet Fitness corporate paint colors, accent wall locations, and approved products?
8. **Existing conditions** ΓÇö New build vs. TI/remodel? Any abatement, existing paint, or patch-and-paint only zones?
9. **Wallcovering schedule** ΓÇö Any WC, or paint-only project?
10. **Specialty coatings** ΓÇö Epoxy floors/walls, intumescent, dryfall, or anti-graffiti required?
11. **Graphics / murals** ΓÇö Painter provides base coat only, or full graphic paint scope?
12. **Site / civil sheets** ΓÇö Any site metal or curb paint in architectural paint scope?
13. **MEP coordination** ΓÇö Paint before or after equipment; access panel and cabinet finish match?
14. **Sheet index** ΓÇö ΓÇ£Full SetΓÇ¥ listed but only A1.01, A2.01, A4.01, AF010 named; request complete sheet index for specs, details, and door/finish schedules.
