## 1) SCOPE OF WORK

Based on the provided takeoff (exterior only):

1. **General**  
   Provide all labor, materials, equipment, and supervision required to complete exterior painting of PT-1 wall surfaces as quantified below, in accordance with manufacturer recommendations and industry standard commercial painting practices.

   Daily and final cleanup of painting-related debris in work areas. Leave surfaces clean and free of paint overspray, drips, and spatters attributable to this work.
